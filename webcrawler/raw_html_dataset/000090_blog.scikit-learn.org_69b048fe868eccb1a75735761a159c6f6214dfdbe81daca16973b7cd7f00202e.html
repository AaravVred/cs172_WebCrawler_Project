    Data Umbrella LATAM Sprint Report

    2021-07-19 less than 1 minute read

    Author: Reshama Shaikh

    Sprint Background

    This sprint was organized by Data Umbrella to increase the participation of underrepresented persons in data science, with a focus on the geographic region of Latin America (LATAM).

    Summary

    The Data Umbrella Latin America scikit-learn online sprint was held on June 26, 2021, and the event report is now available. 40 participants joined from 9 countries.
